CashPilot: feature guide

Proti feature ki kore, kivabe use korbe, bhitore kivabe kaj kore, Free na Pro, ar kon service-er sathe connected
Mul niyom: shob number engine (testable hishab) theke ashe, AI theke na. AI shudhu bhasha-e bujhay. Kono payment, message, reminder ba loan application owner approve na korle jay na. Free: transactions, customers, invoices, dashboard, 7-day forecast, notifications. Pro (14-day free trial): baki shob.

1. Account and onboarding Free

Ki kore
Signup, login, 3-step setup (business name, currency, opening balance, safety cash level). Notun account-e 14 din Pro trial.
Kivabe
/signup → onboarding → dashboard. Password minimum 8 character. Login-e 2FA on thakle 6-digit code lage.
Bhitore
Password bcrypt hash, short-lived access token + rotating refresh cookie, rate limit (beshi bar bhul password dile block). api/auth.py
Connected
Website (Vercel) → API (Render) → Neon database.

2. Dashboard Free

Ki kore
Ek nojore: cash balance, ei masher net, overdue receivable, credit score, 14-day projected balance chart, "Collect first" list, shortage warning, Today's summary (AI-er shohoj bhasha), alert card.
Kivabe
Login korle-i ashe. Shortage banner-e "See the plan" chap, Cash Advisor-e jabe.
Bhitore
/dashboard/summary. Number forecast + credit engine theke; summary sentence AI likhe, tar number engine-er sathe milano hoy.
Connected
Forecast, collections, credit score, notifications, Groq/Gemini (shudhu sentence).

3. Cash Advisor Pro

Ki kore
Shortage-er din ar gap bole, ar ta mitanor step shajiye dey: overdue taka tola, slow stock clear, supplier bill delay, kharoch kom. Proti step-e kot taka ashbe, kobe, ki jhuki.
Kivabe
Step approve / mark done / dismiss / reopen. Approve korleo kichu nijer theke jay na.
Bhitore
engines/planner.py: shobcheye shosta ar nirapod step age. Proti step forecast-e abar chalie effect hishab kore. Action log thake.
Connected
Forecast, Collections, Products, Payables engine.

4. Notifications Free

Ki kore
Sidebar-e "Notifications" link, pashe lal unread count. Alert: cash shortage, cash low, overdue customer, late/due invoice, bill due, supplier plan, loan installment, low stock, credit drop, unusual spending, sales drop, lender follow-up, goal deadline, bank reminder, trial. Total 16 rule.
Kivabe
Page-e Unread/All tab, Mark read, Mark all read, X dismiss, "Open" chaple oi page-e jay. Settings-e proti dhoroner alert on/off, urgent alert email.
Bhitore
alert_rules.py (proti rule alada, ekta fail korle onnogulo cholbe), notify.py (dedupe key + cooldown, tai ek alert bar bar ashe na), daily job email pathay.
Connected
Ledger, loans, products, credit, goals; Mailjet (urgent email).

5. Transactions Free (bank import: Pro)

Ki kore
Income/expense list, filter (money in/out, category, date, search), manual add, CSV import, receipt scan, bank import.
Kivabe
Add: amount, category. CSV: date,amount,type,category,description column. Scan receipt: chhobi upload, form pre-fill hoy, tumi confirm korle save. Connect a bank: Plaid sandbox (user_good/pass_good).
Bhitore
Receipt: Tesseract OCR, na thakle Gemini vision. OCR shudhu suggestion. Bank import: Plaid, pending ar onno currency skip, duplicate ek-i transaction duibar na.
Connected
Forecast, credit score, reports (shob transaction-er upor nirbhor kore). Plaid, Gemini/Tesseract.

6. Cash Flow 7 din Free · 30 din Pro

Ki kore
Protidin-er projected balance, shortage-er prothom din, upcoming payments calendar, what-if simulator.
Kivabe
7/14/30 din toggle. What-if-e sales ba kharoch bodle chalale base vs scenario chart.
Bhitore
engines/forecast.py: opening balance + tarikh-wala bill/loan/recurring + prediction-er sales ar running cost. Safety level-er niche gele flag. whatif.py forecast-ta bodlano input-e abar chalay.
Connected
Transactions, Ledger, Loans, Recurring expenses.

7. Ledger Free (recovery ranking: Pro)

Ki kore
"They owe me" (customer-er invoice) ar "I owe" (supplier-er bill). Invoice add, payment record (partial-o), mark paid. Reminder draft kore pathano.
Kivabe
Reminder: draft toiri → text edit → channel (WhatsApp/Email) → Send. Provider connected na thakle "Recorded as sent, CashPilot did not deliver it" bole copy button dey, fake "sent" dekhay na.
Bhitore
messaging.py: Email Brevo → Mailjet → SMTP, WhatsApp Meta ba Twilio (WHATSAPP_PROVIDER). Fail hole draft thake, provider-er nijer karon dekhay.
Connected
Customers, Suppliers, Collections, Notifications; Mailjet, Twilio/Meta WhatsApp.

8. Customers Free (credit limit, risk: Pro)

Ki kore
Customer list, profile (invoice, payment history, risk level), add ar edit (pencil icon ba "Edit details"), credit limit.
Kivabe
Phone +8801XXXXXXXXX format, WhatsApp-er jonno. Phone/email khali korle muche jay, name khali howa jabe na.
Bhitore
engines/collection.py: Collection Priority Score 0-100 (amount 30%, aging 25%, payment history 15%, cash-gap 15%, ...). Phone/email database-e encrypted (Fernet).
Connected
Ledger, reminders, recovery list.

9. Supplier Plans Pro

Ki kore
Boro bill-er karone cash tight hole supplier-ke instalment-er proposal, financing option-er tulona, supplier-ke message-er draft.
Kivabe
Bill select → plan toiri (draft) → message draft → approve → supplier accept/reject record. Nothing auto-send hoy na. Financing offer gulo demo data.
Bhitore
engines/payables.py: protita taka ekbar-i gona, "confirmed" (known) ar "estimated" (forecast) alada. payables_recommend.py: shobcheye shosta step jeta sotti lowest balance safety level-er upore tole. AI shudhu message-er bhasha likhe, supplier-er asol name code diye dhaka.
Connected
Ledger bills, forecast, Groq/Gemini.

10. Products Pro

Ki kore
Proti product-er margin, sales trend, stock koy din cholbe. Quadrant: star / cash cow / slow / dead / new. Push, keep, shrink na clear.
Kivabe
Product add (sku, cost, price), sale record, stock movement. Sale credit-e hole invoice-er sathe jora.
Bhitore
engines/products.py: 30/90 din units, revenue, gross margin, inventory turnover, cash conversion speed.
Connected
Transactions (sales), Cash Advisor (clear-stock step), low-stock alert.

11. Credit Score Pro

Ki kore
300-900 score, 6 factor, proti factor-er point-er karon, score history chart, "How to raise your score" planner.
Kivabe
Notun account-e "only a starting point" box bole ki data lagbe (income, 3 mash record, loan/bill). Planner bole kon kaj-e koto point (jemon overdue taka tola: +N).
Bhitore
engines/credit.py (6 weighted factor, income 500-500,000 scale), credit_plan.py proti kaj-ke formula-te simulate kore point gone. Kono number AI banay na.
Connected
Transactions, invoices, loans, bills. Daily job protidin snapshot rakhe.

12. Loan Center Pro

Ki kore
Loan readiness, safe monthly installment, 8 dhoroner loan (credit score-er min 500-700 shorto, score bhalo hole sosta), What-if, My loans (nijer loan ar installment), lender introduction request.
Kivabe
Match kora offer-e "Request intro": consent tick na korle Save disabled. Request-er status: submitted → contacted → funded, pichone jay na. Kono lender-ke asol-e kichu pathay na, eta tomar record.
Bhitore
loan_catalog.py, engines/loan.py (evaluate, emi, recommend). Loan figure approval na, estimate.
Connected
Credit Score, cash flow, Referrals, Lender view.

13. Reports and goals Pro

Ki kore
Masik Profit and Loss, insight (ki bodlalo ar keno), PDF/export download, goal (jemon "Emergency reserve") deadline soho.
Bhitore
engines/insights.py + api/reports.py. Goal deadline kachhe ashle notification.
Connected
Transactions, Notifications.

14. AI Agent Pro

Ki kore
Sadharon bhashay proshno: "Will I run short next week?". Tomar nijer number diye uttor.
Bhitore
ai/agent.py: bounded tool-use loop. AI tool call kore engine-theke number ane. AI-r likha jei number engine-er na, seta reject. Free AI na pele keyword intent + template uttor, tai chat shobshomoy chole. Customer/supplier name code kore pathay (Settings-e AI privacy: cloud, local-only, off).
Connected
Prai shob engine, Groq, Gemini.

15. Settings Owner only

Business
Name, currency, safety cash level.
AI privacy
Cloud / local-only / off.
Plan
Free ba Pro. Billing demo mode (kono payment hoy na).
Team
Staff add (name, email, password). Staff shudhu Transactions, Ledger, Customers dekhe. Owner remove korle login sathe sathe bondho.
Lender access
Lender-er jonno expiring, revoke-yogyo link. Token ekbar-i dekhay. Tumi je jinish tick korbe shudhu seta dekhte pabe.
2FA
Authenticator app (TOTP) on/off.
Notifications
Kon alert chai, urgent email.
Data
Download my data (JSON), Delete account (type DELETE, sob muche jay).

16. Lender view Public link

Ki kore
Lender /lender e token boshale owner-er dewa tick-kora tothyo-i dekhe. Revoke/expire hole "Access token invalid" dekhay.
Bhitore
Token hash kore rakha, consent record, audit log.

17. Quick add forms Free

Ki kore
Ledger, Products, Supplier, Plans page-e hater kaje add korar form. CSV na diye ekti ekti kore dewa jay.
Bhitore
components/QuickAdd.tsx shared form, proti form sorasori API call kore.

18. Daily job Automatic

Ki kore
Roj shokale: credit score snapshot, action plan refresh, alert rule chalay, urgent email pathay, cash crunch ashle owner-ke alert. Dui bar chalaleo ekbar-i alert.
Bhitore
jobs/daily.py + scheduler.py. Render free sleep korle shomoy-moto na-o chalte pare (free hosting-er shimana).

19. Security Always on

Ki ache
bcrypt password, rotating refresh token, optional 2FA, contact field encryption, PostgreSQL row-level security (ek business onno-r data dekhe na), audit log, rate limit, data export, full account delete, secret shudhu Render Environment-e.
