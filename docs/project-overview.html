CashPilot

Project overview: ki ache, kothay ache, kon ta kar sathe connected

1. CashPilot ki

Choto business-er jonno AI financial assistant. Business-er records (sales, kharoch, baki, bill) theke cash shortage-er agey warning dey, ke taka dey nai ta list kore, ar credit score ar loan-er hishab dey.

Shob number deterministic hishab theke ashe, AI theke na. AI shudhu bhasha-e bujhay, ar tar bola kono number engine-er sathe na mile reject hoy. Kono payment, message ba reminder owner approve na korle jay na.
