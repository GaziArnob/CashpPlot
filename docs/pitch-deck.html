With no clear, explainable score, owners cannot see what holds them back from finance.

CashPilot3
Solution

One web app, built for owners who are not finance experts.

1. Cash flow forecast

Projects the daily balance 7 to 30 days ahead, flags shortage days, and a what-if simulator tests a loan, a sales change or a new expense.

2. Cash Advisor

Turns the gap into an ordered action plan: collect, clear stock, delay a bill, cut spending. Each step shows its cash effect and risk.

3. Smart payment recovery

Ranks customers by priority, sets credit limits, and drafts reminders for WhatsApp or email. Sent only after the owner approves.

4. Supplier payment plans

Proposes instalments for a big bill, compares financing, and drafts the message to the supplier.

5. Credit score and loans

Explainable 300 to 900 score, a planner for raising it, readiness checklist, 8 loan types tied to the score, consent-based lender introductions.

6. AI financial agent

Ask in plain language. The agent calls the engines and explains the result. Product insights, reports and alerts complete the picture.

CashPilot5
Differentiation

The engines calculate. That is what makes the answers trustworthy.

Deterministic core, AI interface

Every number comes from tested engines. The language model never calculates, and any figure it adds that the engines did not produce is rejected.

Explainable by design

Each credit score shows the factors behind it, and a planner re-runs the real formula to show how many points each action earns.

Privacy first

Customer and supplier names are replaced by codes before any hosted model sees them. Owners can choose cloud, local-only or no AI.

Consent-based lender access

Lenders see only what the owner shares, through scoped, expiring, revocable links. Every view is logged.

Safe agent

Read-only tools, the business taken from the session, a limit on tool steps, and the owner confirms any action.

Zero paid AI services

Free-tier Gemini and Groq with automatic fallback, local models for offline use, free open-source OCR, and free-tier hosting.

CashPilot6
Credit and lending

Explainable credit for businesses with no credit history.

Score built from the business's own records

  • 300 to 900 from six weighted factors, such as income, cash stability and repayment record
  • Every point traced to a reason, for example "overdue receivables lowered your score by 38 points"
  • New accounts see exactly which data to add, never an unexplained low score

A path to a better score and cheaper loans

  • "How to raise your score": each step shows the points it earns
  • 8 loan types, with better scores opening cheaper loans
  • Safe monthly instalment and readiness checklist, an estimate and not an approval
  • Introduction requests need the owner's consent and are tracked to funded
CashPilot7
Trust and security (built)

Financial data deserves real safeguards.

Accounts

Hashed passwords, short-lived tokens with rotating refresh cookies, optional authenticator-app two-factor sign-in, rate limits.

Data isolation

Per-business checks on every query, plus PostgreSQL row-level security so one business can never read another's rows.

Privacy

Encrypted contact fields, names masked before AI, audit log, data export and full account deletion.

Roles

Staff logins see only transactions, ledger and customers. Only the owner manages settings.

Lender access

Expiring, revocable, scoped tokens shown once. Consent recorded.

Quality

More than 260 automated tests cover the engines, the API and the delivery layer.

We do not claim certifications or penetration tests.
